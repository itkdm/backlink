## 费用与收录方式

获批后的基础收录免费；提交前必须在官网首页或页脚放置并验证 “Featured on AInexfinder” 徽章回链，平台会定期复查，移除徽章可能导致下架。另有付费推广选项，具体价格以官方定价页为准。

## 投稿要求

仅适用于具有实质 AI/ML 能力、可公开访问且可实际使用（或处于开放测试）的软件工具或服务。提交需提供官方工具名称、可用 HTTPS 网址、简短介绍、准确的详细介绍、Logo、定价类型、相关分类和联系邮箱；表单当前要求详细介绍至少 250 词。不得提交损坏链接、垃圾或误导内容、重复仿冒项目、没有明确 AI 功能的工具。

免费提交前必须在产品官网首页或页脚添加 “Featured on AInexfinder” 徽章并通过验证；Logo 需为有使用权的正方形图片，建议至少 128 × 128。官网称审核目标为 24–48 小时，但时间会变化且不构成保证；最终由编辑团队审核。

## Fees and Listing

The standard approved listing is free. Before submitting, add and verify the “Featured on AInexfinder” badge backlink on your homepage or footer. The site periodically rechecks it, and removing it may lead to delisting. Paid promotion options are also available; see the official pricing page for current prices.

## Submission Requirements

For software tools or services with meaningful AI/ML capabilities that are publicly accessible and usable (or in open beta). The form asks for the official tool name, working HTTPS URL, short and accurate long descriptions, logo, pricing type, relevant category, and contact email; it currently requires at least 250 words for the long description. Broken links, spam or misleading content, duplicate copycats, and tools without a clear AI component are rejected.

Before a free submission, add the “Featured on AInexfinder” badge to the product homepage or footer and pass badge verification. Use a square logo you have rights to, at least 128 × 128 recommended. The site gives a 24–48 hour review target, but timing varies and is not guaranteed; the editorial team makes the final decision.
