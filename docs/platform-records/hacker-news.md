## 投稿方式与资格

在提交入口发布 story；Show HN 标题必须以 “Show HN” 开头。项目须由投稿者亲自制作、具有实质内容，并能让其他人实际试用。纯博客文章、注册页、newsletter、清单、其他纯阅读内容和募款项目不属于 Show HN。官方建议尽量让用户无需注册或邮件验证即可体验。Show HN 会先进入最新列表，达到一定 points 门槛后才会出现在 Show 栏目。

Hacker News 当前提示正在临时限制 Show HN；建议先熟悉社区并成为有贡献的成员，再偶尔发布。提交页面要求先登录。

## 社区规范

不要把 Hacker News 主要用于推广，也不要请朋友为投稿拉票或评论。官方 Show HN 投稿建议要求作者亲自撰写发布文字，不使用 LLM 生成或编辑。

## Submission and Eligibility

Submit a story through the submission form; a Show HN title must begin with “Show HN.” The project should be substantial, made by the submitter, and usable by others. Blog posts, signup pages, newsletters, lists, other reading material, and fundraisers are not eligible for Show HN. The guidelines recommend making the project easy to try, ideally without signup or email barriers. Show HN stories first appear in the newest feed and need to clear a points threshold to appear on the Show page.

Hacker News currently says Show HNs are temporarily restricted and recommends getting to know the community and becoming a good contributor before posting an occasional Show HN. The submission form requires login.

## Community Rules

Do not use Hacker News primarily for promotion or ask friends to upvote or comment. The official Show HN posting tips ask authors to write their submission text themselves, without LLM generation or editing.
