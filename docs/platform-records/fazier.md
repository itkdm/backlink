## 费用与收录方式

Basic 免费方案标示在 30 天内审核并收录；投稿者需在自己网站首页或页脚添加 Fazier 回链，方案提供嵌入徽章。首页推荐仅在被选中时展示。

Lite 为一次性 $29，可立即发布或预约，并包含首页推荐、永久产品页和社交媒体推广；该方案不要求回链。Premium 为一次性 $49，另含 15 天站内推广和保证的高权重 dofollow 回链。价格与权益以投稿页当前展示为准。

## 投稿要求

投稿入口的 Submit 按钮会先要求登录 Fazier。Basic 方案的审核和收录周期为 30 天内；Premium 方案说明可获得 15 天站内推广。

## Fees and Listing

The free Basic plan says listings are reviewed and published within 30 days. Submitters must add a backlink to Fazier on their own homepage or footer, and the plan includes an embed badge. Homepage featuring is subject to selection.

Lite is a one-time $29 and offers immediate or scheduled publishing, homepage featuring, a permanent product page, and social media promotion; it does not require a backlink. Premium is a one-time $49 and adds 15 days of on-site promotion and a guaranteed high-authority dofollow backlink. Check the submission page for current pricing and benefits.

## Submission Requirements

The Submit button on the submission page first asks users to sign in to Fazier. The Basic plan gives a review and listing window of up to 30 days; the Premium plan describes 15 days of on-site promotion.
