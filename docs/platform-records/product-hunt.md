## 费用与收录方式

常规产品发布免费；付费推广是单独的广告服务。



## 账号要求

公司或品牌账号不能投稿。新建个人账号需等待一周才能发布；订阅 Product Hunt 官方 newsletter 可立即获得发布权限。

## 投稿要求

使用个人账号提交产品直链，并准备产品名称、简短标语、相关主题和产品描述；描述最多 260 个字符。建议由产品创作者本人发布，不需要 Hunter。

建议准备 240 × 240 的方形缩略图；产品图库推荐 1270 × 760，至少上传 2 张图片后图库才会显示。

## Fees and Listing

Regular product launches are free; paid promotion is a separate advertising service.



## Account Requirements

Company or branded accounts cannot post. New personal accounts must wait one week; subscribing to the Product Hunt newsletter provides immediate access to post.

## Submission Requirements

Submit a direct product URL from a personal account. Prepare a product name, short tagline, relevant topics, and a description of up to 260 characters. Makers are encouraged to post their own product; a Hunter is not required.

Prepare a square thumbnail (240 × 240 recommended). Gallery images are recommended at 1270 × 760; at least two images are needed for the gallery to appear.
