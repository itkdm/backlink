## 费用与收录方式

Verified 免费方案需在官网首页添加徽章并通过验证；官方标示审核与发布目标为 7 天内。Highlight 为 $19/月，Spotlight 为 $149/月；两种付费方案均跳过徽章验证并即时发布。外链在项目保持收录期间有效，付费展示需保持订阅有效。



## 账号要求

投稿入口会要求登录后添加创业项目。

## 投稿要求

面向现代公司、微型创业项目和独立项目。登录后在 Dashboard 添加项目，填写官网网址，平台会用 AI 补全资料；选择 Verified 免费方案需在官网首页展示徽章并通过审核。

## Fees and Listing

The free Verified plan requires a badge on your homepage and approval; the site lists review and publication within 7 days. Highlight is $19/month and Spotlight is $149/month. Both paid plans skip badge verification and publish instantly. Links remain while the startup is listed; paid placements require an active subscription.



## Account Requirements

The submission entry requires signing in before adding a startup.

## Submission Requirements

For modern companies, micro-startups, and side projects. Sign in, add a startup from the Dashboard, and enter its website URL; AI fills in the details. The free Verified plan requires a homepage badge and approval.
