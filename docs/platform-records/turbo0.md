## 费用与收录方式

官网定价页列出 Free 免费方案：产品页永久展示并提供 3 条 dofollow 链接，但投稿方必须在自己的网站添加 Turbo0 回链；该方案标示审核与上架时间为两周内。Basic 为一次性 $29.9（页面同时显示划线价 $49.9），包含 3 条可自定义的介绍区 dofollow 链接，Turbo0 回链可选，并标示提交后立即上架。Pro 和 Review Blog 也是一次性付费方案，当前页面分别列出 $99.9 和 $199；功能与价格以官网定价页为准。

## 投稿要求

Turbo0 主要面向内容创作者及其使用的创作工具，官网分类涵盖视频、音频、图像、排版、文案和网站创建等方向。平台称所有产品都会经过人工审核，以检查资料完整性和可用性。提交入口要求登录后继续。

## Fees and Listing

The official pricing page lists a free plan with a permanent product listing and three dofollow links; submitters must add a backlink to Turbo0 on their own website. The plan states a review and listing window of up to two weeks. Basic is a one-time $29.9 (the page also shows a struck-through $49.9 price); it includes three customizable dofollow links in the introduction, makes the Turbo0 backlink optional, and is listed immediately after submission. Pro and Review Blog are also one-time paid plans, currently shown at $99.9 and $199. Check the official page for current prices and plan features.

## Submission Requirements

Turbo0 focuses on content creators and the tools they use; its categories include video, audio, image, typography, copywriting, and website creation. The platform says every product is manually reviewed for information completeness and usability. Login is required to continue through the submission entry.
